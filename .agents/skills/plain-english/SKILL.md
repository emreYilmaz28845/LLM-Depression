# Write in plain English

Say what happened, what it means, and what the reader needs to do. Use the shortest clear wording that keeps the facts intact.

## Write directly

- Lead with the answer or outcome.
- Prefer short sentences and concrete verbs.
- Use common words when they mean the same thing.
- Address the reader naturally with `you` when useful.
- Keep paragraphs focused on one point.
- Use bullets only when they make the text easier to scan.
- State uncertainty, limits, failures, and skipped work plainly.

## Cut AI and corporate filler

Remove throat-clearing, hype, vague praise, and canned transitions. Avoid phrases such as:

- `It is important to note that`
- `In order to`
- `This comprehensive solution`
- `robust`, `seamless`, `cutting-edge`, or `powerful` without a specific reason
- `leverage` or `utilize` when `use` works
- `delve into`, `navigate the landscape`, `foster`, or `unlock`
- `As an AI`, fake quotations, and fake enthusiasm
- repeated summaries that add no new information

Do not replace these with different filler. Delete them or state the concrete fact.

## Keep technical meaning exact

Do not dumb down code, science, safety rules, or provenance. Keep exact names for commands, files, metrics, states, APIs, and statistical concepts. Define a necessary technical term once if the reader may not know it.

Never make a claim stronger, cleaner, or more certain just to make the prose simpler. Plain English means clear meaning, not missing meaning.

## Prefer these patterns

| Avoid | Prefer |
|---|---|
| `We have successfully implemented a robust solution that enables...` | `Implemented X. It now does Y.` |
| `It should be noted that the test was not executed.` | `I did not run the test.` |
| `Please be advised that authorization is required prior to submission.` | `Get approval before submitting the job.` |
| `The system is designed to facilitate the generation of reports.` | `The system generates reports.` |
| `There are several factors that may potentially affect the result.` | `Three things can affect the result:` |

## Check before sending

1. Put the main point first.
2. Delete words that do not change the meaning.
3. Replace vague claims with concrete facts.
4. Split long sentences.
5. Keep only useful headings and lists.
6. Make actions, owners, failures, and next steps explicit.
7. Read it once as normal speech. Rewrite anything that sounds like a press release, paper abstract, policy memo, or generic chatbot.
